What TCG Modern Index is measuring—and what it isn't.

The basket, the grades, the missing observations and the difference between an index level and a card price.

Alex & Oscar BuyTrigger · Published 3 November 2026

A common starting point

An index puts the price paths of a defined basket onto a common scale. A baseline of 100 means the series starts at 100 points; it does not mean a card costs US$100, or that US$100 buys the basket.

For a simple two-card example with equal starting weights, one price rising 10% while the other falls 10% leaves the index at 100 before any rebalancing. The level tells you about the selected basket and method, not the whole hobby.

What is available in this edition

The public research page has 90 exact card identities, dated Raw market references and original evidence links. PSA 9 and PSA 10 numeric data, transaction counts, card-artwork rights and the full performance series are still being prepared for publication. No private portfolio records are included.

Raw, PSA 9 and PSA 10 stay separate

Different grades can trade differently and have different evidence coverage. Each series needs its own matched cards, dates and missing-data rules. A blank PSA 9 observation cannot be filled with a PSA 10 price. The three series are alternative views, never amounts to add together.

Provider guides are useful reference points, but they are not automatically independently verified completed sales. The page should show which kind of observation supports each figure.

Where this first edition stands

The public product being designed is TCG Modern Index. The starting research catalogue currently contains the original 90-card basket, while ten additions remain unfilled. The existing dated 90-card series is kept separate; changing the heading does not turn it into a 100-card history.

Modern's proposed January 2026 history and Top 50's January 2024-onward history still need validation. A line reconstructed later from today's winners must say so. It should not look like a basket that was actually selected and tracked back then.

What every change needs to explain

A new entrant needs an exact identity, a dated admission and a continuity calculation. Adding an expensive card must not manufacture a return. A missing or stale observation also needs a visible rule, rather than a hidden zero.

As the research grows, readers should be able to inspect basket composition, weights, sources, dates and revisions alongside the chart. TCG Modern Index is a research index project; this website does not offer shares in an investable fund.
