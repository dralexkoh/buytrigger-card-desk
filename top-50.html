PRICE NOTES

Behind the numbers.

Prices & history

TCGplayer Raw market guides via TCGCSV combine conditions and are not verified individual sales. History starts 8 February 2024, sampled monthly through 2025 and daily in 2026. Earlier data is unavailable. Source gaps stay visible.

Price source ↗

Artwork & exact editions

Catalogue pictures are reference artwork. Each card’s printing and image notes identify any edition differences; pictures are not condition evidence for a seller’s copy.
