Price the exact printing.

The original selection ranks descending by positive Raw USD marketPrice in its dated Pokémon source archive. Daily updates retain that selection and rank, with each newer guide dated separately. Match the product ID and printing. English single cards only; sealed products, lots, nonstandard cards and trophy awards are excluded. History has a start.

January 2024 is unavailable in this source. The earliest archive is 8 February. This edition samples monthly observations in 2024–25 and uses daily observations in 2026. Lines connect recorded points; no intervening prices are created. Missing sampled observations and reviewed quality holds break the line. 05

Keep the artwork honest.

These are real catalogue scans, not the specific card you would buy. When a scan shows a different edition or finish, its note says so. The price is always matched to the printing in the card heading. Artwork remains the property of its respective owners.

06

Raw available. Graded pending.

Graded prices are not connected yet: each PSA 9/10 reference needs its own exact-grade source and date. Transaction-level sales, volumes and population also remain pending. Raw guides combine conditions and exclude buying, grading and selling costs. Unknown is not zero.
