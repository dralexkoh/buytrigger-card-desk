OPEN THE RULEBOOK

Clear rules. Inspectable numbers.

Proposed TCG Modern Index method
01

Equal at the reset

Each of 100 fully priced constituents starts at 1%. Weights then drift with prices. An expensive card does not automatically dominate.

02

Three separate series

Raw, PSA 9 and PSA 10 are alternative price views. Their coverage is stated separately; they are never added together.

03

Prices, before costs

Index performance excludes grading, shipping, selling fees, taxes and spreads. It is not a collector’s net profit.

04

Gaps stay visible

No guessed history, zero-filled missing prices or silent removal of an unpriced card. Source date and last check remain distinct.

05

Changes are dated

Entries, exits and resets preserve index continuity. Review cadence and admission thresholds will be published before launch.

06

History means evidence

January 2026 is the research target. A later release has no January price. Reconstructed history is labelled.

THE CALCULATION

100 → 105. Adding a card still means 105.

Two equal-weight cards rise 20% and fall 10%: the basket gains 5%. A new member enters at a reset without creating a gain.

Before reset: 100 × [(120 ÷ 100) + (180 ÷ 200)] ÷ 2 = 105.

At reset: the new basket starts at the same 105, with equal target weights. A card priced at $1,000 does not add $1,000 to the index.

After reset: if one of three equal-weight cards rises 10% and the other two stay flat: 105 × (1.10 + 1 + 1) ÷ 3 = 108.50.
