Raw methodology v1 · 3 Nov 2026
01

A selected basket

100 exact English issues, retaining the original 90 plus ten disclosed additions. Catalogue order is not a current price ranking.

02

Fixed, visible membership

The January-history basket and current-USD selection keep their original members. Later source gaps do not remove cards or invent a price. The loaded source receipt shows each basket’s base date and coverage.

03

Equal starting weights

Each basket starts at index 100 with equal weights. Weights then drift with prices; no periodic reset is applied in v1. Neither curve claims all 100 cards were priced in January.

04

Gaps stay gaps

Missing daily observations remain blank. A reviewed source anomaly is excluded from charts and returns while its original amount, reason and archive receipt are preserved. There is no interpolation, invented price or currency substitution.

05

Source dates matter

Daily archived TCGplayer guides combine conditions and exclude costs. Identical prices on consecutive days are repeated guide observations, not proof of another sale.

06

Retrospective research

We selected this basket in November and reconstructed its available prices. This is not proof it would have been selected in January, a tradable fund or a collector’s net return.

THE CALCULATION

Equal at the start. Honest about the drift.

Two equal-weight cards rise 20% and fall 10%: the basket gains 5%. Prices then move the weights; v1 does not reset them each day.

Starting basket: equal exposure to a $100 card and a $200 card; index = 100.

First move: prices become $120 and $180. 100 × [(120 ÷ 100) + (180 ÷ 200)] ÷ 2 = 105.

Next move: the first rises another 10% to $132 and the second stays at $180. Using the original quantities gives 111, a 5.71% gain from 105. Weights have drifted.

Membership: this edition keeps its baskets fixed. A later change needs a new version and an explicit continuity calculation.
