The next thousand dollar Pokémon card? We tested five contenders. A question, not a forecast.

Cleffa leads the visual analogy. Its raw sample median is twenty-nine dollars. The PSA ten guide is four hundred.

Eevee is our second lead research case: a warm scene with a recognisable character.

Ninetales has the golden forest. Beautiful artwork still needs lasting buyers.

Gastly brought Haunter and Gengar. The risk is an already expensive illustration rare.

Groudon takes the cinematic route. A memorable picture does not prove a bargain.
